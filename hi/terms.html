Last updated 6 October 2026

These terms explain how my commissions work, so we both know what to expect. By sending a commission request you agree to them.

Ordering

A commission starts when I accept your request and send you a sketch. I may decline any request, and I only take as many orders as the open slots allow.

Payment

  • I charge 50% before I start coloring, after you approve the sketch.
  • The remaining 50% is due when the final art is ready. You receive the files after it is paid.
  • Prices are in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) and may change for new orders, never for orders already accepted.

Revisions

Every commission includes one round of changes at the color draft stage. Fixes to small details are free. Major changes to the pose, composition or character design after the sketch is counted as a new commission or an added fee, which I will quote before I start.

Cancellations and refunds

  • You can cancel before the sketch is approved for a full refund of anything paid.
  • After the sketch is approved, the 50% deposit is non-refundable because work has started.
  • If I cannot finish your commission, you receive a full refund.
  • Once the final art is delivered, the sale is final.

Usage rights

I keep the copyright to everything I create. You receive a personal license: you may use the art as an avatar, print it for yourself, give it as a gift and share it online with credit to me.

Commercial use, such as book covers, merchandise, game assets or advertising, needs the commercial option at checkout (+50% of the commission price). You may not resell the art, claim you drew it, or use it to train AI models. I may show the finished piece in my portfolio and on social media unless you ask me not to before delivery.

Content I won't draw

I do not accept requests for hateful content, real people in harmful or sexual situations, sexual content involving minors, or anything illegal. I can end a commission that breaks this rule and refund the unfinished portion.

Delays and communication

If something delays your order, I will tell you as soon as I can. If I do not hear from you for 14 days during a commission, I may close it, and the deposit stays with me for the work already done.
